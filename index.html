I study how people interpret health information in digital environments and how AI systems influence health perceptions and behaviors. My research combines behavioral science and human-computer interaction to design and evaluate responsible AI systems that support informed decision-making and sustainable human-AI interaction.


Keywords: Responsible AI, Health Communication, Human–AI Interaction, Health Behavior Change, Conversational Agents, Large Language Models
